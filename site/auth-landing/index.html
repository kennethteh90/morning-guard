Morning gate

During your window, non-essential notifications are held from the status bar. Alarms, phone calls, and apps you allow can still break through.

Digest, not spam

Held alerts are stored on your phone and listed after your goal. Android does not let apps put them back in the shade—you open what matters from the digest.

Goals that count

Verifiable goals: Bible reading, timed focus, or Health Connect steps. Finish one, then start your day on your terms.

Local first, sync if you want

Notification text and settings stay on device. Optional sign-in syncs streaks and Bible highlights— or stay in local-only mode.

Common questions

Does Morning Guard upload my notifications?

No. Notification title and text for held alerts are stored on your device for the digest and are not uploaded to our servers. See the privacy policy for details.

Do I need an account?

No. Core features work without sign-in. An account is optional if you want cloud sync for streaks and Bible highlights.

Why does this site open when I sign in?

Magic-link email may open in the browser first. This page sends you back to the Android app to complete sign-in—only when the link includes a sign-in code.
