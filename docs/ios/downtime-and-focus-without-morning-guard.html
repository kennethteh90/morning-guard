Without Morning Guard

A calmer morning with Apple settings

You can shape mornings with Screen Time Downtime and a scheduled Focus—no extra app required. This guide is for iPhone’s built-in tools only.

All guides · Using Morning Guard? Other guide

Common setup: morning Downtime to limit apps, the same hours in a Morning Focus for notifications, plus Lock Screen Time Settings and Block at Downtime so limits are harder to brush off.

What you’re combining

ToolWhat it does
DowntimeMost apps unavailable except Always Allowed; good for a morning or bedtime window.
FocusSilences or filters notifications; schedule it to match Downtime.
App Limits (optional)Daily caps on Social, Games, etc. outside your morning block.

When Downtime ends, your apps are available again unless you’ve set App Limits for later in the day.

First: turn on Screen Time

Apple routes schedules through App & Website Activity.

  1. Open Settings → Screen Time.
  2. Tap App & Website Activity → Turn On App & Website Activity.
  3. Optional: Share Across Devices if you use iPad or Mac too.

Morning Downtime

Schedule Downtime for the hours you want phones closed—many people use something like 6:00–8:00 or align with breakfast.

  1. Settings → Screen Time → Downtime.
  2. Turn on Scheduled (or Turn On Downtime Until Tomorrow for tonight only).
  3. Pick Every Day or Customize Days, then set start and end.
  4. A 5-minute reminder appears before Downtime starts.

If you want Downtime to stick

  1. Screen Time → scroll down → Lock Screen Time Settings (passcode).
  2. Downtime → turn on Block at Downtime.

Without Block at Downtime, iOS nudges you but still lets you through. Apple: Downtime schedules

Always Allowed

  1. Screen Time → Always Allowed.
  2. Add Phone, Messages, Clock, Health, and anything you truly need during Downtime.
  3. Add key contacts under Allowed Contacts.

Create a “Morning” Focus

Focus quiets notifications. It does not block apps the way Downtime or Morning Guard shields do.

  1. Settings → Focus → + → Custom → name it Morning.
  2. People — who can still reach you.
  3. Apps — allow essentials; silence social, news, and shopping.
  4. Options — dim Lock Screen or hide silenced notifications if that helps.
  5. Add Schedule — the same times as Downtime (e.g. 6:00–8:00).

From Control Center

  1. Open Control Center → Focus → Morning.
  2. Tap ⋯ for how long it should stay on.

Optional: App Limits later in the day

  1. Screen Time → App Limits → Add Limit.
  2. Pick a category (e.g. Social) and a daily time.

Two starting points

Structured morning

Downtime + Morning Focus

  • Morning Downtime with Block at Downtime + passcode.
  • Matching Focus schedule for notifications.
  • Always Allowed: Phone, Messages, Clock.

Lighter touch

Focus only

  • Scheduled Personal or Do Not Disturb while you read, walk, or pray.
  • Add Downtime later if you want app blocking too.

If something feels off

SymptomWhat to check
Downtime didn’t block appsBlock at Downtime on; Screen Time passcode set; app is not in Always Allowed.
I kept using apps anywayWithout passcode + Block at Downtime, iOS only reminds you—you can continue.
Notifications still loudFocus on and scheduled; check per-app Critical / Time Sensitive settings.
Too strictShorten Downtime window or add apps to Always Allowed.
