With Morning Guard

Your morning, with shields and a goal

Morning Guard blocks the apps you pick until you finish today’s goal. Pair it with a Morning Focus for quiet notifications, and optional bedtime Downtime so you’re not double-locked at wake-up.

All guides · Using Apple settings only? Other guide

Gentle stack: Morning Guard for app shields + goal, Morning Focus on the same hours for notifications, evening Downtime (e.g. 10 p.m.–7 a.m.) if you want help winding down—not the same window as your morning routine unless you want maximum quiet.

Who does what

Role in your routine
Morning GuardShields chosen apps until Bible, focus timer, or steps goal is done.
FocusKeeps notifications calm; you schedule it (the app cannot turn Focus on for you).
DowntimeOptional at night; blocks most apps except Always Allowed.

Morning Guard shields apps you choose; it doesn’t quiet notifications from other apps. A Morning Focus helps keep your Lock Screen calm.

First: turn on Screen Time

Apple routes schedules through App & Website Activity.

  1. Open Settings → Screen Time.
  2. Tap App & Website Activity → Turn On App & Website Activity.
  3. Optional: Share Across Devices if you use iPad or Mac too.

Set up Morning Guard

  1. Install Morning Guard from the App Store, then tap Authorize Screen Time during setup.
  2. In Apple’s picker, choose apps to shield (leave Phone and Messages off unless you mean to block them).
  3. Set your morning window and pick a goal type.
  4. While your morning routine is still active, a shielded app shows Apple’s screen → tap Open Morning Guard → finish your goal → those apps unlock for the day.
Peace break turns app shields back on; it does not silence notifications. Leave Morning Focus on if you still want quiet during a break.

Create a “Morning” Focus

Focus quiets notifications. It does not block apps the way Downtime or Morning Guard shields do.

  1. Settings → Focus → + → Custom → name it Morning.
  2. People — who can still reach you.
  3. Apps — allow Clock, Phone, Messages, and Morning Guard; silence the rest.
  4. Options — dim Lock Screen or hide silenced notifications if that helps.
  5. Add Schedule — match your Morning Guard morning window (e.g. 6:00–9:00).

From Control Center

  1. Open Control Center → Focus → Morning.
  2. Tap ⋯ for how long it should stay on.

Bedtime Downtime (optional)

Let Morning Guard own the morning. Use Downtime for wind-down instead of duplicating the same hours.

  1. Settings → Screen Time → Downtime.
  2. Turn on Scheduled (or Turn On Downtime Until Tomorrow for tonight only).
  3. Pick Every Day or Customize Days, then set start and end.
  4. A 5-minute reminder appears before Downtime starts.

If you want Downtime to stick

  1. Screen Time → scroll down → Lock Screen Time Settings (passcode).
  2. Downtime → turn on Block at Downtime.

Without Block at Downtime, iOS nudges you but still lets you through. Apple: Downtime schedules

Always Allowed

  1. Screen Time → Always Allowed.
  2. Add Phone, Messages, Clock, Health (for steps), and Morning Guard.
If Downtime overlaps your morning window and Morning Guard is not Always Allowed, you may not be able to open the app until Downtime ends.

Example day

Morning Guard + Morning Focus + evening Downtime

  • 6:00–9:00 — shields + Morning Focus.
  • 22:00–6:00 — Downtime, Block at Downtime, Screen Time passcode.
  • Always Allowed — Phone, Messages, Clock, Morning Guard, Health.

If something feels off

SymptomWhat to check
Apps not shieldedScreen Time allowed for Morning Guard; your morning hours include right now; apps selected in the picker; Home still shows your routine as active.
Notifications still loudMorning Focus schedule; Time Sensitive / Critical overrides in app notification settings.
Can’t open Morning GuardAlways Allowed; or Downtime overlapping morning hours.
Feels too strictRemove morning hours from Downtime; keep night-only Downtime.
Remove shieldsSettings → Screen Time → Morning Guard, or clear picker in app. See our privacy policy (Screen Time section).
