## Quick comparison

| Tool | Blocks opening apps | Quiets notifications | Ends when you finish a goal |
|------|---------------------|----------------------|----------------------------|
| **Morning Guard** | Yes (apps you pick) | No | Yes |
| **Downtime** | Yes (except Always Allowed) | Partially | No (schedule only) |
| **Focus** | No | Yes | No (schedule or manual) |

**With Morning Guard:** shields + goal, Morning Focus for notifications, optional **evening** Downtime (not the same morning window).

**Without Morning Guard:** morning Downtime + Focus; add Screen Time passcode and Block at Downtime if you want limits to stick.
